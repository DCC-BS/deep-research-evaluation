# NVIDIA DRA (lean) vs research-agent on the German administrative set

Read-only analysis. Sources: `results/nvidia-dra@lean/de` (11 reports, 8 judged: R1 R2 R3 S2 S3 W1 W2 W3), `results/research-agent/de` (13 judged), `logs/search.jsonl`, NVIDIA prompts in `candidates/nvidia-dra/prompts/orig` and the 2.2 source. No model or service was called. All paired numbers below use the 8 tasks both have judged (NVIDIA 57.2%, research-agent on the same 8: 45.9%, on all 13: 44.0%). Eight tasks and one low-effort judge (GPT-5.4-mini) make this noisy; read effects as direction, not size. NVIDIA is ahead on 6 of 8 tasks (R3 +23.3, W1 +23.4, S2 +30.0, R1 +10.0, R2 +6.6, W3 +3.7) and behind on S3 (-3.3) and W2 (-3.3). The gain therefore comes from the fact-heavy, narrow tasks (law, comparison of four named cantons), not from the broad survey tasks.

## Summary

1. **The gap is one criterion: "every claim has a fitting source".** Mean score (0-3) on common criterion 2 is 0.75 for NVIDIA vs 0.13 for research-agent; research-agent gets 0 on 6 of 8 tasks. Next: limits (c6) 2.38 vs 1.88, structure/completeness (c1) 2.0 vs 1.63, dates (c4) 1.13 vs 0.88, primary sources (c3) 1.38 vs 1.13, language (c5) 2.38 vs 2.13. Task-specific criteria: 1.77 vs 1.48 (31 criteria).
2. **Short and concrete beats long and abstract under this judge.** NVIDIA reports are 4.4k-12.7k characters (median about 8.2k), research-agent 11.5k-65.6k (median about 31k). research-agent carries 37-229 citation markers per report (G3: none), NVIDIA 13-98. The judge rule "unbelegte oder erfundene Zahlen, Paragraphen oder Geschäftsnummern: höchstens 1 Punkt" is hit more often the more sentences there are. Each extra 800-word part is another chance for a wrong claim.
3. **research-agent wastes search effort on a query bug.** 376 of 1228 DE searches (31%) have an unbalanced quote and 399 (32%) return nothing, vs 13% empty for NVIDIA lean (31 of 246). Cause in `research_agent/agent.py` lines 122 and 138 (details in change 1).
4. **NVIDIA's structural edge is a grounded plan plus a constraint-driven, compact write-up**, not depth of research. `lean` caps it at 8 research queries and about 20 web searches and it still wins. Most of this is prompt-level and cheap. The 715k tokens come from re-sent context in an agent loop, which we should not copy.
5. **Both agents mislabel cantonal law as SR.** NVIDIA R1: "IDG, SR 153.260", R3: "BPG, SR 730.100" (Basel-Stadt law is SG). research-agent R3 labels SG 730.100 correctly but writes "Energiegesetz SR 772.100". The judge did not punish NVIDIA for it, but a rule is cheap (change 8).

## 2. Report anatomy

| | NVIDIA lean (11 reports) | research-agent (13) |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 4.4k-12.7k chars | 5.8k-65.6k, median about 31k |
| Headings | title, `Zusammenfassung`, `Hauptteil` with numbered or entity subsections, one comparison or matrix section, `Offene Punkte und Grenzen`, `Sources`: 4-6 H2, 3-7 H3 | 8-11 H2, 10-30 H3, plus summary, per-part sections, `Einordnung und Bewertung`, closing |
| Claims | often a bullet with a hard value: "Überstand maximal 20 cm [2]", "Ersatzabgabe 150 CHF pro kW [3]" | prose paragraphs of 3-6 sentences, followed by an uncited inference sentence ("Daraus folgt ...") |
| Tables | 0-13 table rows per report, 41 rows total in 11 reports, 0 with empty last cell, one matrix over the entities the task named | 297 rows in 13 reports, 24-52 per long report, 30 rows end in an empty cell (e.g. S2 Beleg column) |
| Citations | `[n]` numbered, stacked `[1][2]` in 9 of 11 reports (1-18 times) | `[n]` resolved by code from `[E..]`, never stacked |
| Source list | `Title: URL`, no date | `Title: URL (abgerufen am ...)`, 14-35 sources |
| Limits | own H2 `Offene Punkte (und) Grenzen`, specific items | closing part of 80-180 words, generic ("SR-Nummern ... bedarf einer weiteren Verifizierung") |

Patterns that correlate with higher rubric scores: (a) fewer, official sources fully used (R3 N cites 5 sources, all from bs.ch or grosserrat.bs.ch, vs 14 for research-agent); (b) a matrix over exactly the entities the task lists with a cited cell per fact; (c) identifiers in the text; (d) gaps stated per item. Negative: padding sections, extra entities, sources that do not belong (research-agent R1 cites a job advert `campus-career.ch/pdfs/...` as [14], REHAB Basel [19], a Zug Kantonsrat paper [16]), and uncited analysis paragraphs.

NVIDIA is not clean either. R1 has a broken source list (eight titles without URL, then `## Sources [1] mcp_opencaselaw__search_legislation`), so the judge sees `cited_urls=1` and scores c2 = 0, yet gives c7 = 2. Stacked citations remain the main faithfulness weakness (see `nvidia-quality.md` section 1).

## 3. Process: what NVIDIA does differently

Prompts read in `candidates/nvidia-dra/prompts/orig/{researcher,writer}.j2` and the 2.2 `planner.j2`, `long-form-report-writer/SKILL.md`.

- **Planner discovers before it plans.** It must call the search tools ("Discover what exists (you must use tools)") and uses what it saw to set `answer_strategy` (answer_type, title, `required_components` with id, name, description), `constraints` (explicit requirements plus implicit ones such as language, "maximize specificity: include all user-stated details") and self-contained queries with `preferred_tools` and `fallback_tools`. Evidence in the logs: NVIDIA R3 queries `"25.0921.01" Basel-Stadt Solaroffensive PV-Pflicht` and `Richtlinie Solaranlagen Kanton Basel-Stadt aktuelle Version Bau- und Gastgewerbeinspektorat`: the identifier and the issuing office are known before the researcher starts. research-agent's `PLAN` is written blind and then 48 to 177 searches are made around it.
- **Queries are few and clean.** 8 searches in R3 against research-agent's 71. research-agent appends ` site:a OR site:b ...` (8 domains) to the first query of every sub-question and strips quotes with `.strip('"')`, which turns `"Denkmalpflege" Basel-Stadt` into `Denkmalpflege" Basel-Stadt`. 347 queries carry the site chain, 376 have unbalanced quotes, 399 return no results.
- **Researcher** (1 primary + at most 1 fallback call, `max_researcher_model_calls` default): "Do not answer from model memory ... record a ResearchGap instead", findings carry `evidence`, `confidence`, and the note has `evidence_judgment` (0-100 usefulness, confidence, rationale) and gaps. The writer anchors on high-score notes and uses low-score notes "mainly for gaps, caveats, conflicts". research-agent has no relevance score per card, so a job advert and a hospital page reach the writer with the same weight as the Gesetzessammlung page.
- **Writer**: reads plan and notes, picks the most specific skill, treats `required_components` and `constraints` as "coverage checklist", must state evidence thinness as a limitation, and may only cite what `get_verified_sources` lists. It decides where "dense tables, matrices, timelines" help. The skill asks for 3000-5000 words but Gemma writes 1.0-2.1k; the short outcome is accidental and helps here.
- **Task contract.** Both candidates receive the same appended contract (summary, main part, open points; every fact with full URL; SG/SR, Geschäftsnummer, Stand). NVIDIA satisfies "Zusammenfassung, Hauptteil, offene Punkte" almost literally; research-agent builds its own section tree.
- **MCP routing.** The generated configs add `mcp_opencaselaw` as a function group with the same eight tools research-agent allows. In R1 NVIDIA used 2 web searches and mostly MCP (`gesetzessammlung.bs.ch/app/de/texts_of_law/153.270` as first source), research-agent 35 to 68. The routing advantage is a prompt matter (the planner puts the MCP tools in `preferred_tools`), not a different tool set.

## 4. Cost check

Median total on DE: NVIDIA lean about 715k (range 526k-1.17M), research-agent 251k (25k-465k; embeddings 6M tokens extra are metered separately).

Cheap to replicate (prompt, schema, small code): grounded planning with a light discovery step, constraint checklist, compact writer, matrix with a cited cell per fact, per-item limits, relevance weighting, query hygiene. Together roughly +5k to +15k tokens per run, or a net saving when the query fix and shorter parts reduce calls.

Inherently expensive: the supervisor, planner and writer as tool-calling agents that re-read `/shared/` files and re-send 15-40k context 50 times (`nvidia-tokens.md`: 93% of tokens are prompt tokens, writer phase 183k from one-note-per-call reading). Not worth copying; research-agent's design already rejects it. Note that NVIDIA lean got its 57.2% with only 8 research queries, so breadth of research is not what wins here.

Order to try: 1 (fix first, nothing to argue about), then 2 + 4 + 5 in one preset/prompt run, then 3 and 6, then 7 and 9. Re-judge on the same 13 tasks and watch c2, c6 and mean length. Do not copy: NVIDIA's agent loops, file-based note passing, or stacked citations (which hurt faithfulness per `nvidia-quality.md`); keep one citation per sentence.

Caveats: 8 judged NVIDIA reports (R1 R2 R3 S2 S3 W1 W2 W3), G and S1/ST1 pending; a different judge model or a fetching judge could rank the two systems differently, since this judge only checks plausibility ("plausibel abrufbar"), not the page. The baseline tool-calling agent (53.8%) also beats research-agent, so several of these gains may be about simplicity rather than NVIDIA specifically.
